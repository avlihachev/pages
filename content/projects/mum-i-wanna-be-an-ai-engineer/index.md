**Mum, I wanna be an AI Engineer: The production half of building with agents** is the [course](/courses/mum-i-wanna-be-an-ai-engineer/) as a book. Live on Amazon since 24 October 2026.

- [Paperback](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HP3HK3QK), 7x10, black and white
- [Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HP2SZMXW), no DRM

## What is inside

An opening chapter on why a software factory needs these parts, then the eight modules of the course: inference economics, enforced harness, evals as engineering, observability and cost, retrieval as a component, security and compliance, serving and platform, and the pre-review capstone. Each part ends with an interview angle.

The book is reading-only. Code appears as illustration with its output; the graded exercises, the tutor and the freshness checks stay on the course site, which is free.

## How it is built

The book is generated from the same lesson sources as the site. Book-only edits are anchored on lesson text; the intro and the cover quote numbers from recorded runs, and a drift check in the repository's verify gate fails when a quoted number no longer matches its record or a lesson moves away from the printed edition. When that happens, the edition gets a re-upload instead of a quiet divergence.
